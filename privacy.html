Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brain Brawl ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares information about you when you use the Brain Brawl mobile game and related services (the "Service"), and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Information you give us:

  • Account information, including your display name, email address, country and password. Passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and the email address you allow them to share.
  • Messages you send to our support team, together with your email address. A report about another player also contains the display name and player identifier of that player.

Information created when you play:

  • Game data, including your scores, game results and moves (used, for example, to verify daily puzzle results), experience points, coins, achievements, brain profile statistics, leaderboard rankings, the players you follow and the multiplayer rooms you join.
  • Purchase information, including the items you buy and your Premium status. Payments are processed by Apple or Google, and we never receive your payment card details.

Information about your device:

  • A random identifier generated by the app, which we use to keep your account signed in on one device at a time.
  • The results of app integrity checks provided by Apple or Google, which help us detect modified versions of the app, and other signals that help us detect cheating.

We do not access your contacts, location, photos or camera.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • create and manage your account and save your progress;
  • run leaderboards, multiplayer rooms and other social features;
  • process purchases, provide Premium and respond to refund requests;
  • keep the Service fair and secure, including by detecting cheating, fraud and abuse;
  • check display names against a list of offensive words and review the names that players report;
  • send daily puzzle reminders, which are scheduled on your device and can be turned off in Settings; and
  • respond to your support requests and improve the Service.

Information Visible to Other Players

Your display name, country and game results can be seen by other players on leaderboards and in multiplayer rooms, and by players who follow you. We never show your email address to other players.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us operate the Service, and only to the extent they need it to do so:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and handles account sign-in;
  • RevenueCat, which manages in-app purchases and subscriptions;
  • Apple and Google, which process payments, provide Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, and perform app integrity checks; and
  • Google AdMob, which serves the optional rewarded ads. We request non-personalized ads only, although AdMob may still collect device and usage information as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of our players, the Service or others.

Refund Requests

If you request a refund from Apple, Apple may ask us for information about how you used the purchase. In response, we send Apple, through RevenueCat, details such as how long you have had your account, whether the purchased item was delivered and used, and the total amount of your purchases and refunds. Apple uses this information to decide whether to approve the refund.

We share this information to protect against refund fraud. If you would prefer that we do not share it, email us at hellobrainbrawl@gmail.com and we will stop sharing it for your account.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Security and purchase logs are kept for about 90 days and then deleted.

If we change your display name because it breaks our rules, we keep a record of the previous name for as long as your account exists, so that we can deal with repeated breaches.

If you delete your account, we delete your account and the game data associated with it. We keep only a minimal record, made up of your email address or Apple or Google account identifier and the date of deletion, to prevent abuse through repeatedly deleting and recreating accounts. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own records of purchases under their own policies.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can delete your account at any time from the Account page in Settings.
  • You can turn off reminders in the Notifications section of Settings.
  • Watching ads is always optional.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing hellobrainbrawl@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe that a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

International Data Transfers

Our service providers may store and process your information in countries other than the one where you live, and those countries may have different data protection laws.

Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections and access controls. However, no method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at hellobrainbrawl@gmail.com.
