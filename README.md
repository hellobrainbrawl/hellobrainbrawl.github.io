# Brain Brawl public pages

Served by GitHub Pages at https://hellobrainbrawl.github.io:

- `/` support page (contact + links)
- `/privacy.html` Privacy Policy
- `/terms.html` Terms of Service
- `/app-ads.txt` the ad companies allowed to sell the app's ads (AdMob and its partners check it)

These pages are generated from the app's in-app legal text and must stay identical to it.
app-ads.txt is copied from the app repo (scripts/legal-site/app-ads.txt).
Do not edit the HTML by hand: change the text in the app, regenerate, and push.
